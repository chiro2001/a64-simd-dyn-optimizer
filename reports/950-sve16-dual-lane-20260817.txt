[conclusions]
1. SVE16 dual-lane dct16/dct32 are CORRECT on 950 (all gates PASS, both
   compilers) but substantially SLOWER on real hardware: dct16 ~2.2-3.5x,
   dct32 ~1.5-2.3x slower than upstream SVE / NEON. Static fused_uop win
   (dct16 640 vs op895 952; dct32 897 vs opbase 1129) does NOT transfer
   to cycles.
2. Consistent with the repo's repeated lesson (sao/satd/pure-SVE): low
   static instruction count != real-machine cycles on Kunpeng; dual-group
   TBL/packing ops likely lengthen latency despite fewer total
   instructions.
3. gcc-16 and clang-22 agree directionally; clang-22 slightly closer to
   base but still clearly slower.
4. Not adoptable on 950 as-is. op895/opbase remain the 950 kernel-level
   picks; op4032 (+71% sve, non-bit-exact) remains the policy-gated E2E
   win candidate.
