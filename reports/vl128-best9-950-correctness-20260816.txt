Interpretation
--------------
- Most best9-950 entries are either pure NEON, scalar, or VL-agnostic SVE2;
  they are safe on SVE2-128.
- Three SVE2-specific candidates are NOT safe on VL=128:
  * satd-8 (the SVE2 pack-2 / generic recipe uses svptrue predicates and a
    16-lane packed layout that only works at VL=256),
  * interp8vpp-16/32 (loads a full 16-pixel row with svptrue_b16, which at
    VL=128 carries only 8 pixels and therefore produces wrong output).
- dct8 uses NEON registers + SVE dot via arm_neon_sve_bridge and is VL-safe.
- interp8-16/32 (horizontal sdot-d path) and interp8-vps (NEON inline) pass.

Action
------
For a VL=128 SVE2 machine, do not deploy best9-950 as-is. Either:
1. filter out satd-8/interp8vpp-16/interp8vpp-32 from the SVE2 bundle; or
2. generate VL=128-native variants:
   - satd-8: use the existing pure-NEON best_sve1.cpp / AGO NEON cover,
   - interp8vpp-16/32: replace svptrue_b16 row loads with whilelt/16-byte
     fixed-width loads (the current kernels are written for VL=256).
