  gated via QEMU (VL=256) instead.
  Other gates: dct16/dct32/idct16/idct32 need SVE2p1, interp8 path-B needs SVE2p3 - also

[conclusions]

     diverges >10%; dct8 native (clang) exactly matches docs/31 (NEON 3 / cand 4).
  2. Node is strict SVE1 (SVE2 tofu cadd/tbl2 SIGILL confirmed).
  3. On 920B the only native-perf-measurable kernels are the SVE1-shape substituted ones
     and dct8; all SVE2/SVE2p1/SVE2p3 correctness stays on QEMU.
  4. dct8 cross-machine: 920B native ratio 0.75 (cand slower) vs 950 native 1.483
