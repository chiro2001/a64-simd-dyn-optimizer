SVL:  16 bytes = 128 bits (svcntb)

Experiment (satd8 8x8, batch=4096, samples=20, taskset -c 0, 3 repeats)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Implementation                          median CNTVCT ticks
NEON baseline (x265 primitives.satd)    2141 2141 2141
SVE1 pack-2 (CADD90 emulated via tbl)   2286 2286 2286
SVE2 pack-2 (native svcadd_s16 x8)      1720 1727 1719

Ratios (median of medians)
--------------------------
SVE1 / NEON  = 1.068x slower  (+6.8%)
SVE2 / NEON  = 0.803x time    (1.245x faster, -19.7%)
SVE2 / SVE1  = 0.752x time    (1.329x faster, -24.8%)

Interpretation
--------------
On the same microarchitecture and same 128-bit vector length, native
SVE2 CADD90 removes the SVE1 emulation chain (tbl + sign + mul + add).
SVE2 satd8 pack-2 is ~33% faster than the SVE1 emulated version and
~25% faster than the NEON baseline. This directly supports the ISA
audit in docs/53: SVE1's lack of CADD90 is a real structural penalty,
not just a QEMU/static-count artifact.
